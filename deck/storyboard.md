# Storyboard

74 slides, one per spoken sentence or phrase of `../talk/transcript.md`. The code is `engine/story/s*.py`; each line below is one `tl.beat`. Every slide but the title, Figure 2 (15 to 18) and the thank-you carries a cue plate at the bottom left. Colour: NO red, YES green, the paper's run blue, our run orange, the fact card aqua, unknown grey, highlights yellow; ticks and crosses are ink. The board says where the data come from: white paper on legs for the paper's figures and data, a dark terminal on a monitor stand for the released data recomputed, a white board with an orange rim on a lab bench for our own runs. Each act has one set behind everything that stays put through the act: a coloured wainscot along the back wall in muted colours that carry no meaning elsewhere, and one set piece in steel and slate: wood panelling (1 to 18), a cooler wall, low grey tiles and two glass cabinets with bottles (19 to 40), a warm wall, lilac cork and a world map pinned to it (41 to 46), a pale slate wall and slate boards (47 to 67), stage boards, purple curtains and a valance (68 to 74). Figure 2 (15 to 18, 68) stands at the left with the robot on the floor at its right, inside the frame. The openings of the rerun, the split and the variations (26, 41, 47) dim the lights to an ellipse around the subject; the recompute (19) opens on its three props drawn large. Question cards about places carry a brown pin, so wine stays NO only. Each model keeps one look: GPT-4o mini a pink antenna and a chat-bubble badge, Llama 3.3 a brown beret, DeepSeek V3 a slate fin, Gemini 2.5 Flash a gold bow, GPT-5.6 Luna a gold crescent moon, our labeller a judge's cap.
